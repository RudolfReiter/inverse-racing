02 / UNDER THE HOOD

Learn the objective.
Then look ahead.

The driver’s motion carries clues about its priorities.
Inverse optimal control turns those clues into
interpretable acceleration and jerk penalties.

01 ↗

Observe the motion.

Collect short sequences of progress, speed, and acceleration along a reference path.

Estimate an acceleration envelope and fit two smoothness weights to the observations.

↻

A recreation, in the open.

This is a recreated implementation, not the original repository. It uses a synthetic reference circuit, SciPy solvers, nested weight fitting, and a shared constraint slack when needed. The paper’s original data, KKT-based inverse solver, acados integration, and hardware evaluation are not included. Numerical equivalence to the published results is not claimed.
